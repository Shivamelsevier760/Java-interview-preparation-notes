Image Source Finding the middle value answers a different question than calculating an average. An average adds every value and divides the total by the number of rows, which means unusually high or low values can pull the result away from the center of the data. The median instead orders the values and finds the middle position, making it a good fit for salaries, prices, response times, order totals, delivery times, and other data where extreme values can affect an average.
