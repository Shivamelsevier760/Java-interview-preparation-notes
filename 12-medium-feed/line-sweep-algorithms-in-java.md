Image Source Sweep-based algorithms help with problems where the answer changes only at certain positions, not at every point between them. That idea turns a wide scan into a series of ordered updates. You take the places where something begins, ends, enters, leaves, or intersects, turn those moments into events, sort them, then move through that ordered stream while keeping track of what is currently active.
