# Self introduction new for the interviews

1. Tell me about yourself

Hi, I’m Shubham Tiwari. I’m currently working as a Sales Officer, where I use my background in technology and business communication to build strong client relationships and achieve sales targets. I have a degree in Computer Applications from HRIT Institute of Technology.

At HDB Financial Services, I’ve improved my sales skills while also using my technical knowledge to offer better solutions to clients. Right now, I’m enhancing my technical skills further by doing Cisco’s CCNA training to deepen my knowledge in networking and IT infrastructure.

Outside work, I enjoy playing volleyball and going on solo driving trips.

2. What are your strengths?

I’m good at understanding both business and technical needs, which helps me offer the right solutions to clients. I explain technical ideas in a simple way and use data and market trends to find new opportunities and deliver better results.

3. What are your weaknesses?

Sometimes I take on too much work because I want everything done perfectly. I’m learning to trust my team more, delegate tasks better, and focus on clear communication to work more efficiently.

4. Describe a challenging situation at work and how you handled it.

At HDB Financial Services, I once had a client with very specific technical needs that didn’t match our usual solutions. It was challenging because the client needed a custom approach, and there was time pressure. I listened carefully to their needs, used my technical knowledge to design a tailored solution, and worked closely with my team to deliver it quickly. In the end, the client was happy, and we even strengthened our relationship with them.

5. Where do you see yourself in five years?

In five years, I see myself in a more technical and client-focused role, where I can combine my sales experience with deeper IT knowledge, especially in networking and infrastructure. I also aim to earn certifications like CCNA and maybe CCNP, so I can take on bigger projects and help solve more complex client problems.

6. Why should we hire you?

You should hire me because I have a strong mix of technical and business skills. I understand client needs well, explain technical solutions clearly, and find ways to add real value. Plus, I’m continuously upgrading my technical knowledge with certifications like CCNA, and I’m committed to growing and contributing to the company’s success.

7. Give me an example of a time you had to work closely with a team to achieve a goal.

At HDB Financial Services, we had a target to launch a new loan product within a short deadline. I worked closely with the sales and operations teams to align on client needs, technical details, and paperwork. By communicating clearly and dividing tasks smartly, we launched the product on time and even exceeded our initial sales targets.

8. Describe a time you failed. How did you handle it?

Early in my career, I lost a potential client because I focused too much on the product features and not enough on their specific needs. I realized my mistake after the meeting. I handled it by following up with the client, asking for feedback, and improving my approach. Since then, I always focus first on understanding the client’s problems before offering any solution.

9. Tell me about a time when you had to adapt to change.

When new digital tools were introduced at HDB Financial Services, many processes shifted online. At first, it was challenging because it changed how we interacted with clients. I quickly adapted by learning the new tools, helping my team understand them, and adjusting my sales approach. It actually helped me work faster and serve clients better.
